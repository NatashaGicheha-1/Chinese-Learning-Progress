2. Xiànzài shì xiàwǔ.
   * 现在是下午。
      * Now is afternoon.
> Better version below.

       It is afternoon now.

3. Yīfu zài zhuōzi shàng.
   * 衣服在桌子上。
      * The chair is on top of the table X
      * The clothes are on the table.✓

4) Yīshēng zài yīyuàn.
   * 医生在医院。
      * Doctor in hospital 
> Better version below.

       The doctor is at the hospital.

5) Xièxie nǐ
   * 谢谢你。
      * Thankyou
> Better version below

       Thank you.(Thank you is two words)

6) Xuéshēng zuótiān xuéxí
   * 学生昨天学习。
      * The student studied yesterday.✓

7) Zhōngwǔ wǒ zài xuéxiào
   * 中午我在学校
      * My school is in China X
      * At noon I was in school.✓

8) Xiǎo háizi yǒu yīfu.
   * 小孩子有衣服。
      * Small child swim chair X
      * The small child has clothes.✓

9) Zhè shì wǒ de zhuōzi.
   * 这是我的桌子。
     * Where is my table? X (almost there)
     * This is my table. ✓

10) Wǒ zuò huǒchē qù xuéxiào.
school
   * 我坐火车去学校。
     * I go to school by train. ✓ (Not attempted)

# Lesson 1:Basic Greetings in Mandarin Chinese
- For beginners, basic conversation in chinese - spoken language - theme based
- Greetings
- Ni hao - hello/hi/ how are you, response Ni hao
- Ni hao ma - hello
- Hao - good
- Hao ma - is it good?
- Hao jio bu jien - long time no see
- Hen - very
- Hen hao - very good
- Yie - also
- Ni ne - and you
- Na - particle used in analytical questions
- Ni - You
- Wo - I/me
- Xiexie - thankyou
- Zaijian - goodbye/ see you again

- Chinese is a musical language
- There are 4 tones
