# 🏮 Christmas in China

Christmas is not a traditional Chinese festival.

Instead, it has become primarily:

- Commercial
- Social
- Urban
- Trendy

---

## Where It Is Most Visible

Major cities:

- Beijing
- Shanghai
- Guangzhou
- Shenzhen
- Chengdu

---

# 🍎 Christmas Eve (平安夜)

## Ping'an Ye

Christmas Eve is often more significant than Christmas Day.

### Peace Apples (平安果)

A unique Chinese tradition.

People exchange apples as symbols of:

- Peace
- Safety
- Good luck

---

# 🎄 Conclusion

Although Christmas originated as a Christian celebration, its meaning continues to evolve globally.
In Western societies it remains closely tied to faith and family traditions, while in China it has developed into a modern, commercial, and social event, especially among younger urban populations.

🎄❤️🏮 *A global holiday with local meaning.*
